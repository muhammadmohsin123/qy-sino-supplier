02 / Company

One order. One supplier.

QY Sino Supplier is a general order supply business on Jhang Road, Muzaffargarh. We source and deliver the materials, consumables and equipment that contractors, factories and institutions need to keep work moving.

We work from your list, not a fixed catalogue. A single purchase order can combine cement and steel with pipes, electrical items, safety gear and camp supplies. We consolidate it, source each line from reliable manufacturers and distributors, and deliver to site with clear documentation.

Jhang Road gives us direct reach to Multan, D.G. Khan, Layyah and the rest of South Punjab, and our sourcing network covers sites further afield.

Vision

A trusted multi-category supplier for infrastructure, industrial and institutional projects across Pakistan.

Mission

Turn every requirement list into on-time, on-spec deliveries at fair and transparent prices.
